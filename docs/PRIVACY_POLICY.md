---

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the **Public Policy Intelligence Platform** (`https://sna-executive-policy-dashboard.streamlit.app/`) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit or interact with our research web dashboard.

### 2. Information We Collect
- **Publicly Available Content:** We only process public social media posts, comments, hashtags, and public news articles relevant to specific national policy programs (such as *Makan Bergizi Gratis*, *Sekolah Rakyat*, and *Koperasi Desa Merah Putih*).
- **No Private Data Collection:** We do NOT collect, harvest, or request private account credentials, private messages, contact lists, financial details, or sensitive personal data.
- **Analytics & Aggregate Metrics:** All user interactions and posts are anonymized and grouped into aggregate statistics (total volume, sentiment scores, and network community clusters).

### 3. How We Use Information
The collected public data is strictly used for:
- Academic research and thesis evaluation at Universitas Jenderal Soedirman.
- Training and benchmarking IndoBERT Natural Language Processing (NLP) models for 3-class sentiment and 9-dimension emotion detection.
- Constructing anonymized Social Network Analysis (SNA) community graphs to understand issue diffusion.
- Generating aggregated visual charts and executive research reports.

We **do not sell, monetize, rent, or distribute** any collected data to commercial third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

### 4. Third-Party API Compliance (TikTok, Meta, Google, X)
We strictly adhere to the developer policies and data protection standards of all integrated services:
- **TikTok for Developers:** When using TikTok APIs, data access is limited strictly to authorized public scopes. We do not attempt to bypass access restrictions or de-anonymize TikTok creators.
- **Data Retention & Deletion:** Cached public content is periodically refreshed or purged in accordance with platform retention rules. If a user deletes their public post on the original platform, it is removed from our database during synchronization.

### 5. User Rights and Data Deletion Requests
If you are a content author whose public social media post is included in our academic dataset and you wish to have it removed or anonymized, you may contact us at any time. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.
